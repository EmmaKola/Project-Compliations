### 1. NexaCart Global E-commerce Performance Analytics
 - **About Project** - This project delivers an end-to-end commercial performance analysis for NexaCart Global, a multinational e-commerce company operating across 20 countries and 14 product categories. Using approximately 25,000 orders from 2020 to Q1 2026, the analysis evaluates revenue trends, order volume, average order value, product performance, return rates, acquisition channels, membership tiers, and geographic demand. The project combines a relational data model, KPI-led visual analysis, an executive summary, and department-specific recommendations to help commercial leaders protect recent growth, improve customer value, reduce returns, optimise channel investment, and prioritise high-potential markets.<br>


 - **Tools** - Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power BI, DAX, GitHub/Markdown

 - **Operations Performed** - Data cleaning and validation, Relational data modelling, ERD development, KPI creation, Time-series and variance analysis, Revenue and AOV analysis, Order-volume analysis, Product and return-rate analysis, Customer acquisition analysis, Membership-tier segmentation, Geographic performance analysis, Data visualisation, Business insight, Department-specific recommendation

 - **Process Involved** - Defined the commercial questions and north-star metrics, prepared and connected the orders, customers, product summary, and monthly revenue tables, validated keys and relationships, created calculated measures for delivered revenue, order volume, AOV, return rate, growth, and benchmark variance, then analysed performance across time, products, customer segments, acquisition channels, and countries. The findings were converted into clear charts, an executive summary, and practical recommendations for the sales, product, marketing, returns, membership, and country-management teams.

### 2. Nova Med Solutions Performance Analytics
